FEED THE BLOB

Loot rains from the sky. Your blob is hungry. So are the others.

  1. Grab loot and carry it to your green blob.
  2. Tap RUN or press Space to lunge. Lunge into rivals to make them drop loot.
  3. Lunge into a rival blob to steal goo. It flies home by itself. Three hits in a row is a jackpot, then run.
  4. Defend your own blob when the alarm goes off.
  5. Explore the big map: follow the loot clouds, smash crates and use the portals.
