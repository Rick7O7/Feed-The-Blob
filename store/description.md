## Short description (one line)

Grab falling loot, feed your blob and raid your rivals to grow the hungriest blob on the map.

## Long description

Loot rains from the sky, and four hungry blobs want all of it. You are the little collector of the green blob. Grab apples, donuts, pizza, crystals and gold, carry them home and watch your blob grow from a tiny Droplet into a planet-eating giant.

Your rivals will not wait. Lunge into their collectors to knock the loot out of their hands, or charge their blob itself: three hits in a row steal a jackpot of goo. Get out fast, because an angry blob blows up and grows spikes.

Every half minute the whole arena changes: Gold Rush with falling meteors, a Boss Blob that eats everything in its path, Speed Frenzy, Low Gravity and a Loot Tornado. Spend your coins on eight upgrades, wear funny skins and hats, finish quests, come back daily for a growing gift and rebirth into five different worlds with stronger rivals.

Features:
- Quick to learn: move, grab, carry home, lunge.
- 11 blob evolutions per run, endless levels after that.
- 5 chaos events, boss fights and raids on rival blobs.
- 8 upgrades, 26 skins and 6 hats, quests and a 7-day daily streak.
- Rebirth into 5 worlds: Meadow, Lagoon, Glacier, Volcano and Deep Space, each with its own music.
- Plays on desktop and mobile, progress is saved in the browser.

## Controls

Desktop:
- Move: WASD or arrow keys, or hold the left mouse button
- Lunge and sprint: Space, Shift or right mouse button
- Buy upgrades: keys 1 to 8 or click
- Shop: B
- Music on/off: N, sounds on/off: M

Mobile:
- Move: touch and drag anywhere (floating joystick)
- Lunge and sprint: RUN button
- Upgrades and shop: tap
