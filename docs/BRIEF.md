# Brief: why this game exists and the rules around it

Owner: Paul (Germany, Brandenburg). Agents decide and deliver finished work; Paul does only what needs a human (sign up, pay, submit, play-test).

## Goal and frame
- Long-term goal set by Paul: a business that could reach 10 Mio. € revenue per month. Honest math says portal ads cannot get there (would need about 8.1 billion plays per month, 27 times all of CrazyGames). The milestones below are what counts.
- Budget 50 € total, under 5 hours per week of Paul's time.
- Rules from Paul: no selling to colleagues or acquaintances; no product that already exists in better form; every number checked, estimates marked as estimates; tell him early when something will not work; follow German law.
- Per answer at most 3 tasks for Paul, each under 30 minutes.

## Plan
- Studio for browser games, distributed through game portals that pay a share of ad revenue. First portal: CrazyGames. First game: Feed the Blob (based on Paul's own Roblox idea; the Roblox version stays his separate project).
- Revenue estimate (single old data point, an estimate): about 1.23 € per 1,000 plays on CrazyGames.

| Milestone | Date | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First euro in the developer account | by 2027-02-28 | one game in Full Launch |
| 1,000 € per month | Nov 2027 | about 810,000 plays per month |
| 10,000 € per month | Nov 2028 | 8.1 million plays per month |
| 100,000 € per month | Nov 2030 | 81 million plays per month |

- Kill rule: missing the first two milestones in a row, or two games in a row failing the CrazyGames trial, ends the idea.

## CrazyGames facts that shape the game
- Basic Launch (trial): 7 to 21 days, limited audience, no ads, no revenue. Judged on average playtime, conversion to gameplay and retention compared with other games. Only then Full Launch with ads and revenue share.
- Limits: initial download up to 50 MB, PEGI 12, no external ads, no external login.
- Ads: break ads only at natural pauses, the SDK allows at most one every 3 minutes; rewarded ads only on request, never on the running game screen, always with an alternative and an equally large "continue without" button; no revive ad on every death; no do-nothing ad buttons during the trial. Real-money purchases are invite-only.
- Payout minimum 100 €.
